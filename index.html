Associate Professor · Zhizhen School, Chinese University of Hong Kong

I work in representation theory, arithmetic geometry, height functions and related analysis / statistics. I received my PhD from MIT in 2022 supervised by Wei Zhang.

Attached is my CV. Research topics:
  • Lie theory: explicit Langlands program, stacky approach and transfers of integrals
  • Number theory: L-functions, heights, arithmetic power series, theta series, BSD conjecture
  • Explicit arithmetic geometry: moduli spaces, lattices, sheaf theory and special functions / loci
  • Group theory: Galois / Triangle groups, Riemann surfaces (metrized graphs), elliptic / Hitchin fibrations

    • Special functions and their integrals / volumes (with arithmetic kernels) on geometric spaces have fundamental applications. Still only a few are completely proved, with unavoidable local ramifications and archimedean analysis. I aim to find new features, good directions and explicit quantitative solutions on problems in Diophantine / automorphic world (+ analogs).
